    The code behind each step

    Two signed documents travel off chain: the KYC credential and each payment authorization. The chain only answers two questions, using the same two contracts as the degree scenario: is this key one of Maria’s, and has Bank A revoked her KYC?     What changes for the customer

    KYC happens once. Opening a card at another bank takes a credential and a signature instead of a second ID upload, and a lost phone means a new key, not a new card number.

    Teaching model

    Card networks today authenticate payments with tokens, 3-D Secure and passkeys, not DIDs. Reusable KYC credentials and wallet-based payment approval are being piloted, for example around the EU Digital Identity Wallet. The roles and checks here are the same ideas, simplified.
